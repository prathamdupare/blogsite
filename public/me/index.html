me

I’m Pratham — a math graduate from IISER Bhopal who ended up writing code full time. Most of what I make is open source: habit trackers, self-hosted dashboards, and small tools that scratch my own itches. I run NixOS, live inside Neovim and tmux, and care a lot about software that respects the people using it.

This blog is where I write about Linux, Android, open source, privacy, and whatever I’m learning at the moment.

Likes — Linux · open source · Android · reading · music · privacy · minimalism

Dislikes — closed source · surveillance · bloatware
